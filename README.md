# Privacy Policy for FNS SMS (Shuttle Management System)

**Last Updated:** August 29, 2026

FNS SMS ("we," "our," or "us") operates the FNS SMS (Shuttle Management System) mobile application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. 

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information. By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**FNS SMS does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data or personally identifiable information.** 

* **No Personal Data:** We do not ask for or collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials.
* **No Device Data:** The App does not access your device's contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location history.
* **No Usage Data:** We do not track how you use the App, nor do we collect performance logs, crash reports, or device identifiers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics tools, tracking software, or advertisement networks (such as Google AdMob, Firebase, or Facebook SDK). Because we do not use these services, no third parties have access to your data through this App.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our App is safe for users of all ages. Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, we are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We advise you to review this page periodically for any modifications.
